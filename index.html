I'm Nihar, a software engineer focused on reliable systems, infrastructure, and product engineering. I like turning ambiguous problems into simple, durable software that can grow with the people who use it.

Currently I'm a Senior Systems SDE at Sifive in the build systems group. My work has spanned site reliability, cloud infrastructure, operations, security, and full-stack development. I'm especially interested in distributed systems, developer platforms, and pragmatic engineering teams.

Earlier in my career, I was a graduate student in Computer Engineering at Boston University and an engineering intern at Deloitte, working on PowerShell and MySQL scripts for database and operating-system audits.

Outside work, I read science fiction, play guitar, and keep an eye out for the next interesting system to build.
